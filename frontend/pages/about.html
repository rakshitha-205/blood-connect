About BloodConnect

Every drop connects a life.

BloodConnect links patients who urgently need blood with nearby, compatible donors, so families spend less time making calls and more time with the people they love.

  • 8blood groups matched
  • 4steps from request to donor
  • 100%free to use
  • 24/7requests and alerts

Our mission

In an emergency, finding the right blood group nearby often depends on who you happen to know. BloodConnect replaces that search with a simple network: donors share their blood group, location and availability, and patients get a ranked list of donors who can actually help.

Built as a societal project

BloodConnect is a community project, free to use for donors and patients. It is built to make voluntary blood donation faster, safer and more transparent for everyone involved.

How it works

  1. 1

    Join

    Register as a donor or a patient. Donors add their blood group, location and availability.

  2. 2

    Request

    A patient creates an emergency request with the hospital, blood group, units and urgency.

  3. 3

    Match

    We find compatible, available donors and rank them by distance, blood group and availability.

  4. 4

    Connect

    Nearby donors get an alert, call the patient and head to the hospital to donate.
